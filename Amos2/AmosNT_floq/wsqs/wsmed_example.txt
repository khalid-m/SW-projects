Example 1: (with two web service providers)

 I. Import WSDL URLs by selecting the following URLs from the drop-down list and  then click on the Import WSDL button.

   a).  http://terraservice.net/TerraService2.asmx?WSDL
   b).  http://codebump.com/services/PlaceLookup.asmx?wsdl


 II. Example SQL queries:

   a). Find places start with 'Atlanta'

     select gl.City ,  gl.PlaceTypeId from   GetPlaceList gl where   gl.placeName='Atlanta' and gl.MaxItems=100 and gl.imagePresence= 'true'

   b). Find all states information in US

       select * from  GetAllStates gs
   
   c). Find information about places located within 15 km from each city whose name starts with 'Atlanta' in all US states 
       (Two web service operations GetAllStates, and GetPlacesWithin involved in join)

        select gp.TOPLACE ,  gp.TOSTATE, gp.GEOPLACEDISTANCE_DISTANCE from   GetAllStates gs, GetPlacesWithin gp where  gs.State=gp.state and gp.distance=15.0 and gp.placeTypeToFind='City' and gp.place='Atlanta'

   d). Find geographical information about places located within 15 km from each city whose name starts with 'Atlanta' in all US states
       (Three web service operations GetAllStates, GetPlacesWithin and GetPlaceList involved in join)

   select gl.City ,  gl.PlaceTypeId, gl.LON,gl.LAT from  GetAllStates gs, GetPlacesWithin gp, GetPlaceList gl where gs.State=gp.state and gp.distance=15.0 and gp.placeTypeToFind= 'City' and gp.place= 'Atlanta' and gl.placeName=gp.ToPlace + ' , ' + gp.ToState and gl.MaxItems=100 and gl.imagePresence= 'true' 
     
       Above query contains dependent web service operations. GetPlacesWithin depends on the GetAllStates and GetPlaceList depends on GetPlacesWithin.
